## Business operations (build order by dependency)

1. Inventory (Kho) — parts stock with unit cost, auto-deduct on order, low-stock alerts.
2. Job pipeline + checklist — orders as work-orders: stages, build checklist, ETA, assignee, progress photos.
3. Finance — revenue / AOV / paid-vs-outstanding; real profit (revenue − parts cost − labor); deposits, invoices, refunds.
4. Notifications + payments — Zalo/email order-status updates; deposits & invoices; QR/MoMo; shipping tracking.
5. Portfolio + promotions + analytics — admin-managed Archive gallery; coupons; analytics (best-sellers, turnaround, repeat rate).
6. Loyalty — points ledger; earn on order Finished; redeem as checkout discount; tiers.
